Judaeo-Arabic: Arabic written in Hebrew script, the everyday and scholarly language of Jewish communities across the Islamic world for over a millennium. from Saadia Gaon to the Cairo Geniza

A rare pairing

Most translators can give you the words. Fewer can tell you what a document means under Jewish law — and fewer still hold the standing to rule on it.

A dayan sits as a rabbinic judge on a beit din, hearing and ruling on matters of Jewish law.
  • OrdinationRabbinic ordination (semikha), with further qualification as a dayan — authorized to rule on matters of Jewish law, including business disputes, inheritance, and personal status.
  • DoctoratePhD in Jewish Languages, specializing in Judaeo-Arabic — the linguistic and textual history of Arabic written in Hebrew script across the Sephardic and Mizrahi world.
  • FocusReading, translating, and contextualizing Judaeo-Arabic texts — from communal responsa to personal letters and legal documents — for scholars, archives, and families.
This page covers translation, consulting, and teaching work. Halachic rulings are handled separately, through the usual channels of a beit din.
